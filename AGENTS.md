The whole app uses one global light theme: light palette tokens live in :root in src/index.css (no html.light scoping needed — those selectors are now plain `html`), so every page including auth/dashboard/settings shares the same clean white-and-teal look. The homepage additionally has its own .home-light section styling and the uploaded product photo via a CDN asset pointer; particles stay on interior pages only so the homepage remains static and clean.
